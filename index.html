Live paper trading · server-side on Vercel

loading…

Fully automated paper account. A GitHub Actions runner trades 24/7, ticking every minute against real Polymarket books with a fixed paper bankroll, and saves this ledger every few minutes. Every quote must be fully collateralised from that bankroll (BUY YES at bid + BUY NO at 1 − ask ≈ $1 per share pair), filled inventory ties capital up, and markets whose min size the bankroll can't cover are skipped. A fill is booked only when the live book trades through the paper price; rewards accrue from the quadratic score vs live competition and settle at 00:00 UTC — a market's day is forfeited if it earned under $1, exactly like Polymarket's payout floor. Every 6 h the allocator closes idle markets and re-funds the best safe ones, recycling realised P&L. Fill flow is counted only as a cost, never as profit. To pause, change the bankroll or start over, edit paper-config.json in the GitHub repo (running / capital / resetId). No keys to Polymarket, no orders.

Risk controls & kill switches

Paper first. Run the Paper Live tab (or python paper_trader.py) for at least a week; compare its reward pace with what you'd expect before risking capital.
Start on the safest market. python live_test.py picks the Scanner's safest pick and dry-runs the bot against its live book — no keys.
Dry-run by default. The bot prints intended orders until you set LIVE=1. Keys stay in your environment — never in this site.
Dead-man switch. Live mode sends order heartbeats every 5 s; if the process dies, Polymarket auto-cancels resting orders ~10 s later.
Jump gate. Cancel when a mid move exceeds 4× the slow EWMA or fast vol ≫ slow vol; cool down N minutes (v4).
Event guard. Pull quotes N hours before resolution and ahead of scheduled jumps (kick-off, debates, data releases).
Inventory caps. Soft cap → skew + taper; hard cap → stop adding on that side. Merge YES+NO pairs back to USDC daily.
Two-sided integrity. Outside [0.10, 0.90] one-sided quotes score zero — never leave a lone order out there.
Reconcile. Compare modelled share vs /rewards/user/total each day; a persistent gap means your C estimate or uptime is off.
Jurisdiction. Check Polymarket's terms and restricted-region list for where you live before trading live.

Generated config

Scoring per Polymarket liquidity-rewards methodology (quadratic S, two-sided Qmin, c = 3, per-minute sampling, UTC-day epochs). Competition C is estimated from the public book and cannot see order-level min-size filtering or other makers' uptime — treat ref APR as an upper bound. “Safest” ranks lowest adverse-selection risk per reward dollar; it is not a guarantee against loss. Paper fills are conservative (trade-through only) and paper rewards are modelled, not paid. Strategy Lab is a stylised Monte Carlo, not an L2 replay. Research tooling, not investment advice.
